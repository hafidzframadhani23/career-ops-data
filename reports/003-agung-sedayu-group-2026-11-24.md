## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Key Responsibilities
Field Supervision & Execution:
 Monitor daily construction and maintenance of estate infrastructure—including roads, drainage/channels, earthworks, retaining structures, and utility trenches—against approved drawings, schedules, and quality standards.
Contractor & Vendor Coordination:
 Coordinate daily work activities with contractors, subcontractors, and surveyors; verify work progress, material deliveries, and equipment readiness on site.
Quality Assurance & Handover:
 Conduct routine site inspections, check work quality prior to sign-off, resolve technical issues in the field, and support as-built documentation and testing/handover records.
Safety & Compliance:
 Enforce site safety (K3) rules, traffic/access management around active work zones, and environmental compliance during construction and estate operations.
Reporting & Cross-Team Alignment:
 Prepare daily/weekly progress reports, flag potential delays or scope variations early, and coordinate with engineering, project management, estate/township management, and utility stakeholders.
Qualifications & Requirements
Bachelor’s degree (S1) in Civil Engineering or a closely related engineering field.
Proven experience supervising civil or township/estate infrastructure works (property/real estate or large-scale contractor background is a strong advantage).
Ability to read and verify engineering drawings, site layouts, BOQ/volume checks, and basic surveying/leveling outputs.
Strong field leadership, contractor management, problem-solving, and clear communication skills in Bahasa Indonesia (English is a plus).
Willingness to work full-time 
on-site
 across project/township locations and travel to site areas as needed.
