About the role
An Estimator in Building Construction role focused on performing accurate quantity take-off based on drawings and specifications, preparing BOQs and cost estimates, and coordinating with internal teams, suppliers, and subcontractors to identify scope gaps and cost risks.
Key responsibilities
Perform accurate quantity take-off based on drawings and specifications
Prepare BOQ, unit rate analysis, and cost estimates
Review architectural, structural and civil drawings
Evaluate and compare supplier and subcontractor quotations
Identify scope gaps, discrepancies, and potential cost risks
Coordinate with internal teams, suppliers, and subcontractors
About you
Minimum Bachelor's Degree (S1) in Civil Engineering
Minimum 2 years of experience as an Estimator or Quantity Surveyor in building construction
Strong ability to perform quantity take-off, prepare BOQs, conduct unit rate analysis, and prepare cost estimates
Proficient in English (verbal and written) for preparing reports and proposals, with the ability to read and understand technical drawings, specifications, and tender documents
Proficient in Microsoft Excel and familiar with estimating/take-off software
Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and ability to work within tender deadlines
Good knowledge of construction methods and materials
Proficiency in Cubicost by Glodon, AutoCAD, or other estimating/take-off software
Experience in medium to large-scale building construction projects
